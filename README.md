Hi, I'm Fabiana

I want to bring AI to social good and social impact. This is where I share the code of the tools I build for NGOs and public-interest teams.

I am not an engineer. I code with LLMs, and I can follow what the code does thanks to the Applied AI Bootcamp by allWomen. Each repository says how it was built.

What I work on:

- AI for knowledge and advocacy work in NGOs Tools that help small teams find, read and compare policy documents: semantic search, topic classification and summaries, always with a person checking the output.

- Responsible AI for small organisations Trainings and practical guidance for non-technical teams: prompting, the EU AI Act and ethics.

- EU policy, participation and institutions; the field I come from: how EU decisions are made and how civil society can influence them.
  
Background

MA in European Public Affairs (Maastricht) and LLM (Bologna). Previously at the Council of the EU and as a consultant for a UN youth office. Currently leading the team on participation, democracy and EU institutions at the European Youth Forum.
